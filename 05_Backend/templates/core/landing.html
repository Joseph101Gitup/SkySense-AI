Core Intelligence

AI Capabilities

Specialized machine learning features tailored for ground-level atmospheric intelligence.

Image Analysis

Deep visual inspection extracting cloud optical thickness, texture gradients, luminance, and vertical development patterns.

Rainfall Classification

Categorizes cloud specimens into 3 discrete meteorological regimes: Low to Medium Rain, Medium to Heavy Rain, and No to Low Rain.

Confidence Estimation

Computes exact Softmax probability distributions, quantifying prediction certainty to prevent overconfident forecasts during ambiguous regimes.

Historical Analysis

Searchable, filterable archive of past predictions with date ranges, classification filters, and latency auditing.
