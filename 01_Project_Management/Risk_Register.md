| ID | Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Incomplete or low-quality dataset | High | Medium | Start data collection early and validate samples weekly. | Data Lead | Open |
| R2 | AI model accuracy below target | High | Medium | Run repeated experiments and tune hyperparameters before integration. | AI Lead | Open |
| R3 | Backend integration delays | Medium | Medium | Define interfaces early and test integrations in small increments. | Backend Lead | Open |
| R4 | Hardware or sensor availability issues | Medium | Medium | Prepare fallback simulation paths and confirm device access early. | Hardware Lead | Open |
| R5 | Scope creep during implementation | Medium | Medium | Track priorities in weekly planning and review changes with stakeholders. | Project Lead | Open |
| R6 | Documentation and reporting delays | Low | Medium | Maintain a rolling documentation update checklist each week. | Documentation Lead | Open |

## Review Cycle
- Review risks every week during the project meeting.
- Close or reclassify items once mitigation actions are completed.
